This is Spot's Android app (Expo, React Native). The site and the API it calls live in the repo root; the app reads `/api/resolve`, `/api/sniff`, `/api/holdings`, `/api/order` and `/api/execute` from `https://www.usespot.fun`.

## Expo has changed — do not trust your training data

Expo ships breaking changes every SDK release. Before writing code that touches an Expo, EAS or React Native API, read the major version of `expo` in `package.json` and check the matching versioned docs (`https://docs.expo.dev/versions/v<major>.0.0/`) or the package's own README and types in `node_modules`.

## Commands

```bash
npx expo install <package>  # resolves SDK-compatible versions (EXPO_OFFLINE=1 where the network is fenced)
npx tsc --noEmit            # typecheck
npx vitest run              # the plain-TypeScript logic (src/logic.ts, src/trade.ts, src/wallet/base58.ts)
npx expo export --platform web   # the screens in a browser, share simulated by ?url=…&text=…
```

## How it is put together

- React Navigation, not Expo Router: `src/navigation.tsx` holds one screen, Home (the bag, three round keys, the coins sniffed lately), in a native stack, with Resolve, Coin and Choose pushed over it and Connect, Deposit (Add cash), CashOut and Settings (the wallet, every trade, the choices, behind Home's puppy) as sheets: transparent modals that rise over the screen they belong to, with their own motion (`Sheet` and `useRouteSheet` in `src/components/ui.tsx`). A share navigates to Resolve from wherever the app is; Resolve draws the coin's outline while it reads, and the coin fades in over it. Connecting to ape from a coin reads the bag at once and, short of USDC, the same sheet becomes Add cash.
- State the screens share lives in `src/store.tsx` (React context over AsyncStorage, on the phone only). The bag comes from `useHoldings` in `src/holdings.ts`.
- Share → Spot comes through `expo-share-intent` (`src/share.ts`); screenshots are read on the phone by `expo-text-extractor` (`src/ocr.ts`). The wallet is Mobile Wallet Adapter (`src/wallet/mwa.ts`), required on first use so a missing native module costs the wallet button, not the app. None of these run in Expo Go: use a development build or the APK from `.github/workflows/android.yml`.
- Trades: the money rules live in `src/trade.ts`, with specs. One trade at a time however fast the taps; a quote far below the price stops for a yes before the wallet opens; a trade whose fate is unknown (slow network, Jupiter timeout, a failure the chain has not confirmed) locks that coin's button while the app watches the wallet, until the order can no longer land. Never offer a second buy while the first may still land; the swap that turns SOL into cash is locked the same way. The waiting itself happens in `src/pending.ts`, outside any screen and saved on the phone: the user can leave the coin, go Home or close the app, and the verdict (a toast, the activity line, the bag) reaches them wherever they are through `TradeWatch` in `App.tsx`.
- `src/soon.ts` holds what the app may only promise once it is real (the 2x auto-sell, pings). A flag goes on in the build that ships the thing, not before.
- Files ending in `.web.ts` stand in on the web export, which exists to look at screens.
- `android/` and `ios/` are generated by `npx expo prebuild` (Continuous Native Generation). Never edit them by hand; configure through `app.json` and config plugins.

## Design and words

- Benefit first: every screen opens with what the user gets (the verdict, the bag's worth, scams dodged), the details after. One loud element a screen; the rest quiet.
- Simpler wins: a screen keeps what its job needs and folds the rest behind one line, to open in place (the receipt's clean checks past the landing's four, trades past the latest five, tracking an address, the deposit address while a swap can do). Nothing that is a reason or a risk is folded. And a screen says each thing once: a line doesn't repeat the key over it, the figure beside it or the card under it (one Sniff again, one US notice, one SOL figure, the wallet named in one row).
- Pure black ground (OLED), one orange for actions, the white receipt for the sniff. Green buys and red sells on the trade buttons, as every trading app has them; elsewhere colour says the outcome: green is a win (the CLEAN stamp, a sell that made money), red is NOPE, orange is Spot's own moment. A loss is never red. Every colour, size, corner and timing comes from `src/theme.ts` (`color`, `type` with its `leading` and `tracking`, `space` on a 4-point grid, `radius`, `size`, `motion`, `layer`); a screen names what it needs there rather than typing a number. The parts are in `src/components/ui.tsx`: lists are `Group` and `Row`, not a card per row; a trade sits in a `Dock`; anything that rises over a screen is a `Sheet`.
- No all-caps labels and no monospace outside the receipt and wallet addresses; section headings are sentence case.
- Two orchestrated motions: the verdict (checks tick in, the stamp lands at the top of the receipt in the place kept for it, the phone feels it) and the moment a trade lands (`src/components/Moment.tsx`: orange bursts up, the puppy, without its tile on a ground that is already orange or green, bounces in and barks, the word comes up, the phone thumps in rhythm; Share turns it into the card in `ShareCard.tsx`, Done or any tap puts it away). Everything else moves only when touched, and springs as Apple's do (`motion` in theme.ts: a response and a damping ratio): a sheet arrives critically damped, follows the finger by its top and leaves at the speed it was thrown. Reduced motion turns all of it into fades.
- Words: plain CT (ape, bag, dev, rug, honeypot, GRRR, NOPE), dollars on buttons ("Ape $25", "Sell 50% for about $15"), every sniff question asked so "No" is the good answer, errors say what happened and what to do. Words that depend on data live in `src/logic.ts` with a spec: the dock's key and the line under it, the keypad's hints, Add cash's key and lede, the moment's lines. CT words stay in headlines and keys; where a decision or money turns on a word, the plain one is there too ("the coin's address (CA)", "Bad price right now", "about $13", "is back in your wallet as cash"). After an ape nothing shouts: holding the coin and short of cash, Add cash is quiet. A safe answer is the key that stands out ("Cancel" over "Ape anyway").
- Money comes in and goes out outside Spot, and the app says how. Add cash offers a card buy through the wallet app's own Buy (Spot has no card partner) and the address for an exchange or another wallet, each folded to one line; back from the wallet app it reads the bag again. Home's Cash out says the way through an exchange, its network risk in the caution colour. There is no ramp partner and none is planned: the owner ruled out MoonPay on 2026-10-20, as there is no company to sign one. These steps stay.
- Any amount can be typed: Other opens the keypad, for dollars to ape or dollars of the holding to sell. A coin's face is tried through gateways that answer (`faceSources`), and a coin without one gets a quiet letter, never the orange.
