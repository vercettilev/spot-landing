## Description

See a coin on X, TikTok or Telegram? Share the post to Spot.

Spot finds the coin in it and sniffs it before you buy: is it a honeypot, can the dev freeze it or print more, is the pool big enough, is there a tax on selling. One word back: CLEAN, GRRR or NOPE. Honeypots never make it into your bag.

Then ape in one tap, from your own wallet. Spot builds the trade through Jupiter and your wallet approves it. Spot never holds your coins or your keys.

What you get:
• A sniff report on any coin, in plain words. "No" is the good answer.
• Paste a CA, a $ticker or a link, or share a screenshot: Spot reads the coin out of it.
• Ape with your USDC, or straight from your SOL when the USDC runs short: one approval.
• Your bag, priced, with what every ape made since you aped.
• Every trade written down from your wallet's own numbers, a tap from Solscan.
• The CA under every coin, so a lookalike never fools you.

Sniffs are free. No account, no email: everything Spot remembers stays on your phone.

Not financial advice. Coins can go to zero with or without a trick, and the sniff can miss one.
